# Combat and Territory Ideas

Brainstorm for the combat and expansion phase. Nothing here is a rule. Ideas chosen for testing move to [trial-rules.md](trial-rules.md), and from there to [game-rules.md](game-rules.md) if they work.

## Victory (later)

The trial uses total domination through elimination (see [T9](trial-rules.md#t9-elimination-and-victory)). Other options, kept in case it drags:

| Option | How it works | Feel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Total domination** | The first player who dominates the whole board wins. Fits a game that snowballs. | Clear, but the end can drag if the loser holds out in a corner. |
| Territory threshold | At the start of your turn, if you hold at least N cells, you win. | Ends sooner than total domination. |
| Fixed rounds | After R rounds, the player holding the most cells wins. | Predictable length. |
| Points | Cells + buildings + reward cards, first to N points. | Flexible, less focused on the board. |

## Brakes against a runaway leader

The game will probably snowball. Brakes to try if it gets out of hand:

- **Event cards against a chosen player:** the player who reveals the card picks the victim, so in practice it hits the leader. Examples: _Desertion_ (the chosen player loses 2 troops from a cell of your choice), _Bad harvest_ (the chosen player discards 2 grain). Cards are left out of the sandbox and will be designed later to fit the final mechanics.
- **Troop upkeep:** at the start of your turn, pay 1 grain for every 3 troops; lose 1 troop per unpaid grain.
- **Growing building costs:** each new castle costs +1 stone for every castle you already own.
- **Hand limit:** at the end of your turn, discard down to 10 resource cards.
- **Defense advantage:** defender wins ties and buildings give bonuses (already in trial, see T6).

## Discarded ideas

Kept here so they are not proposed again by mistake.

- **Cell control by building influence** (castle 2, settlement 1, port 1): replaced by "only troops count" for production and the "hold a cell" condition.
- **Production for every building next to the cell:** replaced by troop majority (T3).
- **Wagon and ship as pieces on the board:** replaced by the Wagon and Ship actions (T4).
- **One player's troops per cell:** replaced by shared cells (T2).
- **Fight when entering an enemy cell:** fighting is a separate Attack action (T6).
- **Castle siege counted over turns:** hard to show in the UI. Replaced by the Conquer action (T7).
- **Each troop moves once per turn:** removed; troops move as long as the player pays grain (T4).
- **Port combat bonus:** ports give no bonus, so promoting to a port is a trade-off (T6).
- **Loser loses the difference (attrition):** replaced by all or nothing, where the attacker chooses how many troops to commit (T6).
